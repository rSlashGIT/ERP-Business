## Component Integration Workflow

1. **Identify Component Need**
   - What component do you need? (button, card, form, etc.)

2. **Use 21st.dev Prompt**
   - Say: "Add a [component] from 21st.dev"

3. **Customize for Your Brand**
   - Adjust colors, spacing, fonts

4. **Integrate into Project**
   - Claude will handle the integration
